🤖 Robot Commands

These are the specific functions you can use to control Wall-E.

  • walk_forward() - Moves forward 1 space.
  • turn_left() - Turns 90 degrees to the left.
  • turn_right() - Turns 90 degrees to the right.
  • jump() - Jumps up or down a ledge while moving forward.
  • light() - Lights up a blue tile (or turns it off). Light them all to win!

🔁 For Loops

Use a for loop when you know exactly how many times to repeat an action. The i acts as the iterator (a built-in counter).

for i in range(4):
    walk_forward()
    light()

🔄 While Loops

Use a while loop to keep repeating as long as a condition is true. You must manually increase your iterator (i) so the loop eventually finishes, otherwise your game will freeze in an infinite loop!

i = 0
while i < 3:
    jump()
    i += 1
